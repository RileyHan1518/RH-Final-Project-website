About My Research and Academic Interests

As someone interested in both engineering systems and human performance, I enjoy research that lets me combine both areas. My current research focuses on Olympic weightlifting performance, data analytics, and mechanical engineering applications related to design and prototyping.

Research / Academic Interest List

  1. Olympic Weightlifting Trends
    • Max Lift Tracking: Studying historical and recent max lift records to identify trends.
    • World Record Progression: How performance levels have changed over time.
    • Influences: The impact of equipment, training, and weight classes on results.
  2. Gender-Based Comparison in Sports
    • Men vs. Women: Data-based analysis of performance gaps and overlaps.
    • Strength-to-Weight Ratios: Exploring how performance differs across bodyweight classes.
    • Representation: Examining how gender inclusion has evolved in Olympic lifting.
  3. Engineering and Data Tools
    • Using PyScript & Plotly: Building dashboards for interactive weightlifting data exploration.
